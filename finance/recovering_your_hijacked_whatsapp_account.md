# What should I do if my whatsapp account got hijacked?

If your WhatsApp account has been hijacked, **act quickly**. The hacker likely tricked you into sharing a 6-digit registration code or accessed your voicemail. 

Follow these exact steps immediately to recover and secure your account:

---

### Step 1: Re-register your WhatsApp account immediately
1. Open WhatsApp on your phone.
2. Enter your phone number and tap **Next**.
3. WhatsApp will send you a **6-digit code via SMS or phone call**. 
4. Enter the 6-digit code.
5. **As soon as you enter the code, the hacker will be automatically logged out of your account on their device.**

> **Note on Two-Step Verification (2FA):** 
> * If the hacker enabled a Two-Step Verification PIN after hijacking your account, WhatsApp will ask you for this PIN. 
> * Since you don't know it, you will have to wait **7 days** to reset the PIN and access your messages. 
> * **However, entering the SMS code still logs the hacker out immediately**, so they can no longer use your account while you wait out the 7 days.

---

### Step 2: Warn your contacts (Crucial)
While you are trying to recover your account, the hacker may message your friends and family pretending to be you (usually asking for money, gift cards, or another 6-digit code).
* **Call, text (SMS), or post on social media** to inform your close friends and family that your WhatsApp has been hacked.
* Tell them **not** to send money or click any links sent from your WhatsApp number.

---

### Step 3: Check Linked Devices (Once you regain access)
1. Open WhatsApp on your phone.
2. Go to **Settings** (iPhone) or tap the **3 dots** in the top right corner (Android).
3. Tap **Linked Devices**.
4. Look at the list of active sessions. If you see any device you don't recognize, tap it and select **Log Out**.

---

### Step 4: What to do if you CANNOT get the SMS code
If the hacker has requested too many codes and WhatsApp says you must wait hours, or if you cannot log in:

1. Send an email to **support@whatsapp.com**.
2. **Subject line:** `Lost/Stolen: Please deactivate my account`
3. **Email body:** Include your full phone number in full international format (e.g., `+1-XXX-XXX-XXXX` for US/Canada, `+44-XXXX-XXXXXX` for UK).
4. Deactivating the account stops the hacker from using it. You have 30 days to re-activate it before it is permanently deleted.

---

### Step 5: How to prevent this from happening again

1. **Enable Two-Step Verification (2FA) immediately:**
   * Go to **Settings > Account > Two-Step Verification > Turn On**.
   * Choose a 6-digit PIN that only you know and add a recovery email address.
2. **NEVER share your 6-digit WhatsApp code:** WhatsApp will *never* ask you for this code, and no real friend will ever need you to send them a code.
3. **Protect your mobile voicemail:** Hackers often get into WhatsApp by requesting a "Call Me" verification code late at night, letting it go to your phone’s voicemail, and hacking your weak voicemail PIN. Contact your mobile carrier to secure or password-protect your voicemail.
